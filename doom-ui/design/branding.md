# DOOMFLY branding

Created 2026-09-05.

## Logo

The user-selected side-view fly from `public/pixel-fly.png` replaces the original
top-down glyph. `fly-mark.mjs` frames out empty margins using an SVG viewBox,
exports PNG, Apple touch icon, and ICO variants, and embeds display-sized PNGs
in the SVG marks. This is a decorative brand mark, not an anatomical rendering
or simulation readout. The existing illustration is reused; no new image was
generated for this replacement.

Final assets: public/fly-logo.svg, public/fly-logo.png, public/fly-icon-96.png,
public/apple-touch-icon.png, public/favicon.ico. The legacy favicon.svg also
uses the same fly mark.

## Social card

Final asset: public/og.png (1738×905 PNG). Created with the built-in imagegen
tool, one request, no retries. Reference: the original top-down fly-logo.png.
This existing social card is unchanged by the side-view logo replacement. The title and
supporting copy were inspected and match. Used as the site's Open Graph and X
large-image card. No scientific data or gameplay is represented in this image.

### Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: one landscape social sharing card for X large-image cards and Meta link previews.
Primary request: Create a cohesive premium black-and-white 8-bit Fly Brain / Doom social card, target dimensions 1536×800 pixels (approximately 1.91:1).
Input images: Image 1 is the exact newly designed Fly Brain brand logo, a reference image and supporting insert. Preserve its recognizable pixel fly design and use it as a prominent matching white mark on the right.
Scene/backdrop: pure black background with plenty of negative space.
Style/medium: coarse, crisp 8-bit pixel typography and simple hard-edged square geometry. Flat black and white only.
Composition/framing: landscape canvas; dominant title occupies the left approximately 60%; prominent white pixel fly logo occupies the right approximately 30%. Title may break across two lines, with "FLY BRAIN" on the first line and "/ DOOM" on the second. Put supporting text neatly below the title. Add minimal clean 8-bit border corner accents. Keep ALL essential text and the complete logo inside the central 85% of canvas width and central 70% of canvas height, leaving substantial outer black crop-safe margins. The title is the strongest visual element.
Text (verbatim): "FLY BRAIN / DOOM"
Supporting text (verbatim): "166,700 NEURONS. ONE LIVE EXPERIMENT."
Constraints: exact spelling and punctuation; white text on black, clean readable coarse pixel letterforms, reference logo recognizable and faithful; no other copy.
Avoid: website screenshots, browser frames, actual gameplay, fake scientific plots, claims of learning or validated biology, gradients, colorful elements, gray backgrounds, decorative textures, watermark.
